# The roman banners

The two README banners share one style and one set of prompts - kept
here for reproducibility. Regenerate freely; keep the iconography.

## Style commun

Ultra-wide 16:3, dark charcoal, "modern antiquity": black marble,
brass, toga silhouettes, data-stream light (amber + teal), flat
vector minimalism, museum-poster aesthetic, NO TEXT (image models
mangle letters).

## augure (v1) - the augur reads the birds

The augur's iconography: the LITUUS (the curved staff), the TEMPLUM
(the sky divided in quadrants), the birds' flight = the signs read;
the teal star at the convergence = the verdict.

```
Ultra-wide cinematic banner, 16:3, dark charcoal sky at dusk. A
modern Roman AUGUR stands in profile on a high black-marble terrace:
a hooded figure in a dark toga with a thin gold trim, holding a
brass LITUUS - the curved augur staff - raised toward the sky. Above
him, the night sky is divided into four large rectangular quadrants
(the ancient templum, drawn in faint glowing gold lines), and
through them fly BIRDS rendered as glowing amber data-arcs - each
flight path traced with fine telemetry dots like instrument
readings. One quadrant burns brighter: a teal star pulses at the
convergence of the flight paths - the verdict the augur reads. Below
the terrace, a dark circuit-board cityscape glitters. Roman
antiquity fused with precision engineering: marble, brass, toga
silhouette, data-stream birds. Flat vector minimalism with subtle
gradients, high-end museum-poster aesthetic, muted amber/copper/teal
on charcoal, NO TEXT, no letters, no logos.
```

## lictor (v1) - the fasces of fiber, the trail behind

The lictor's iconography: the FASCES (the bound rods + the blade =
the power to execute) rendered in fiber-optic; the luminous
footprints behind = the trail; one door open, the others sealed; the
laurel arch = the judgment carried out.

```
Ultra-wide cinematic banner, 16:3, dark charcoal. A modern Roman
LICTOR walks left to right along a black-marble road in profile: a
disciplined hooded figure in a dark toga, carrying on his shoulder
the FASCES reimagined - a bundle of glowing fiber-optic rods bound
together with a brass laurel band, one thin polished blade
protruding from the bundle. Behind him, the road shows a trail of
luminous teal footprints receding into the dark - every step
recorded. Ahead of him stands a massive dark gate whose mechanisms
(a gear, a lever, a coil) animate faintly with amber light as he
approaches; one door stands open, lit from within. At the far right
edge, a subtle laurel-wreath arch marks the destination. Roman
antiquity fused with precision engineering: marble, brass, toga
silhouette, fiber-optic fasces. Flat vector minimalism with subtle
gradients, high-end museum-poster aesthetic, muted teal/copper/amber
on charcoal, NO TEXT, no letters, no logos.
```
